📸 Screenshots Guide

Google Play: Minimum 2, Maximum 8 screenshots
Apple App Store: At least 1 per device size

Recommended Screenshots:

  • Home Screen - Main discovery feed
  • Product Detail - Show product with price
  • Shop Profile - Boutique page
  • Categories - Browse by category
  • Stories/Arrivages - New arrivals feature
  • User Profile - Account management
  • Search - Product search
  • Cart/Checkout - Shopping experience

📋 How to Capture Screenshots:

  1. Run your Flutter app on a phone or emulator
  2. Navigate to each screen listed above
  3. Take screenshots (use device buttons or Flutter DevTools)
  4. Save as PNG in assets/store/screenshots/
  5. Recommended size: 1080x1920 (portrait)

📤 Upload to App Stores

Google Play Console:

  1. Go to Store Presence → Store Listing
  2. Upload app icon (512x512)
  3. Upload feature graphic (1024x500)
  4. Upload screenshots (minimum 2)

Apple App Store Connect:

  1. Go to My Apps → Select your app
  2. Add screenshots for each required device size
  3. App icon is managed in Xcode (already configured)
